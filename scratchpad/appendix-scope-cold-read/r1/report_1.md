## Scope judgment

A substantial cut is warranted. This chunk alternates between necessary safeguards and a textbook derivation of a familiar diversification-return identity. The essential result is that independent residual simulation omits contemporaneous residual covariance, so matching marginal variances cannot validate portfolio growth. That result needs two paragraphs and a small number of equations, not seven paragraphs of Taylor expansion, second-moment identities, pairwise algebra, and an AR(1) example. The appendix should spend its space on the unusual choices that change interpretation: finite-path cross-terms, horizon centering, different ACF aggregations, the limits of the pass rates, benchmark sample exclusions, and the GRU's restricted comparison scope.

**Recommended budget: 10 prose paragraphs for this chunk, down from 20.** This would leave 20 paragraphs for the other 38 actual prose paragraphs under a whole-appendix ceiling of 30. The chunk supports that ceiling; I cannot certify the allocation of the unseen appendix. Do not achieve the reduction merely by concatenating existing paragraphs. Remove the optional work identified below. Target zero to one floats here, down from four: remove F1–F3 as standalone floats and retain only a selective F4 if its rows supply unique evidence for the generator claims.
