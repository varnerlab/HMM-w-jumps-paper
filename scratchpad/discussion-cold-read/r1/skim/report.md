Reading scope: abstract and introduction as context; discussion.P1–P3 audited in order. Methods and Results were intentionally skipped. The concerns below concern the Discussion's interpretation, organization, and local clarity; they do not request a second Methods section.

Paragraph purposes: discussion.P1 summarizes the reuse benefit, compares distributional performance with alternatives, then appraises tail coverage and the limits of validation. discussion.P2 interprets the limited transferability of the jump-duration improvement from SPY to composed assets and unseen data. discussion.P3 begins by interpreting market exposure and distributional distortion, then combines stress-test mechanics, branch checks, a fallback sensitivity result, and two distinct limitations for portfolio and cumulative-return applications.

The three findings that emerge are: correcting the variance improves daily distributional fit over naive composition while remaining competitive with residual fitting; improving temporal behavior in the single-asset generator does not guarantee improvement after composition or in the holdout; and preserving total variance does not preserve distributional shape, with weaker distributional fit among assets with greater market exposure. These are suitable centers for P2, P3, and P4 of the requested five-paragraph Discussion. A short study summary can form P1, and limitations and further considerations can form P5. The current three paragraphs do not satisfy that requested structure: P1 and especially P3 combine too many purposes, while P2 already has a relatively clear interpretive center.
