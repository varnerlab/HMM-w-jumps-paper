\title{Hybrid Hidden Markov Model for Modeling Equity Excess Growth Rate Dynamics: A Discrete-State Approach with Jump-Diffusion}
\author{Abdulrahman Alswaidan}
\author{Jeffrey Varner\,$^{*}$}
\address{Robert Frederick Smith School of Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering, Cornell University, Ithaca, NY 14853}


\begin{abstract}
\begin{linenumbers}
Empirical equity excess growth rates exhibit heavy-tailed distributions, negligible linear autocorrelation, and persistent volatility clustering that classical Gaussian diffusion models cannot reproduce. We propose a hybrid hidden Markov framework that discretizes continuous excess growth rates into Laplace quantile-defined market states and augments the regime-switching process with a Poisson-driven jump-duration mechanism to enforce realistic tail-state dwell times. Parameters are estimated by direct transition counting, avoiding the Baum-Welch EM algorithm entirely. Applied to ten years of SPY data, the framework achieves Kolmogorov-Smirnov and Anderson-Darling pass rates of 98.5\% and 94.6\% in-sample and 94.0\% and 94.2\% out-of-sample (full calendar year 2025) across 1,000 simulated paths, reproducing the ARCH effect that standard regime-switching models miss. GARCH(1,1), by contrast, achieves only 6.0\% and 1.4\% in-sample pass rates despite its well-known volatility clustering properties. A Single-Index Model extension propagates the SPY factor path to a 424-asset universe, enabling correlated synthetic path generation for portfolio stress testing and scenario design.
\end{linenumbers}
\end{abstract}
