\section*{Code and data availability}
The experiment scripts, evaluation harness (six-composer benchmark,
Value-at-Risk coverage check, and bias-correction utility), and fitted
per-asset marginals are available in the
\href{https://github.com/varnerlab/HMM-w-jumps-paper}{paper repository}.
The underlying model-fitting library is available in the
\href{https://github.com/varnerlab/JumpHMM.jl}{JumpHMM.jl repository}
and is pinned as a dependency of the experiment code.
