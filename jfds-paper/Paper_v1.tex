\section*{Acknowledgments}
The authors thank the eCornell AI in Finance program participants for
discussions that motivated the downstream-use bias-correction analysis,
and Polygon.io for access to the 2014 to 2024 US equity and ETF daily
price histories used in the experiments. Computations were run on a
single laptop; no shared-resource allocation was required.

\paragraph{Author contributions.}
A.A.\ designed the hybrid hidden Markov marginal generator, performed the
single-asset experiments and validation framework, and contributed to the
manuscript. J.D.V.\ designed the variance-corrected single-index
composition, performed the multi-asset experiments and downstream
Value-at-Risk validation, developed the bias-correction methodology,
released the code, and contributed to the manuscript.

\paragraph{Competing interests.}
The authors declare no competing interests.

\paragraph{Code and data availability.}
The experiment scripts, evaluation harness (six-composer benchmark,
Value-at-Risk backtest, bias-correction utility), and the fitted
per-asset marginals are released in the paper repository
(\url{https://github.com/varnerlab/HMM-w-jumps-paper}); the
underlying model-fitting library is released separately as
\texttt{JumpHMM.jl}
(\url{https://github.com/varnerlab/JumpHMM.jl}), pinned as a
dependency of the experiment code.
