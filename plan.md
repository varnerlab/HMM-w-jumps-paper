# Figure, Table, and Theory Plan for High-Impact Submission

## Reviewer's Verdict on Current Figures

The current figures fail the "so what?" test. Every panel is either a density overlay or an ACF
plot — all generated by the same pipeline with minimal differentiation. A hard reviewer at JFE or
Management Science will write:

> *"The authors provide no benchmark comparison, no visualization of what the model learned, no
> quantitative ACF evidence beyond visual inspection, and no multi-asset demonstration. The figures
> are diagnostic outputs, not scientific arguments."*

The redesign needs to make three arguments visually: (1) the problem is real, (2) the mechanism is
sound, and (3) the model wins against alternatives.

### Figure 2 — Model Architecture (1 TikZ diagram)
**Argument: the mechanism is transparent and principled**

A labeled diagram showing:
- The state space {1, ..., N} on an axis with tail states shaded red/blue
- Thin arrows = standard Markovian transitions from T
- A Poisson clock (ε) triggering a jump episode (thick colored arc)
- During jump episode: forced routing to tail-state set for K ~ Poisson(λ) steps
- Emission: small Normal bell curves above each state

This is non-negotiable for a methods paper. Reviewers need to see the logic in one figure.

## Theoretical Content Needed

### Proposition 1 — Laplace MLE (closed form)
State and prove that μ̂_L = median(R) and b̂_L = (1/T) Σ|R_t - μ̂_L|. This is a one-paragraph
proof that justifies the "no numerical optimization needed" efficiency claim.

### Proposition 2 — Consistency of the Counting Estimator
State: under ergodicity of the observed chain, T̂_{ij} → T_{ij} a.s. as T → ∞ by the ergodic
theorem for Markov chains. Cite Grinstead & Snell or Billingsley. This converts an informal
argument in the current Methods into a formal result.

### Proposition 3 — Stationarity of the Augmented Chain
The hardest and most important theoretical result. Prove (or state as a proposition with a sketch
proof) that the hybrid chain — standard Markovian transitions interrupted by Poisson jump episodes
— remains irreducible and aperiodic (hence ergodic, hence has a unique stationary distribution)
provided:
  (i)  T is irreducible and aperiodic
  (ii) 0 < ε < 1

Key argument: between any two states i, j, there is a positive-probability path through the tail
states using the jump mechanism, so irreducibility is preserved.

   largest new computation but highest-impact addition for top-tier submission.
