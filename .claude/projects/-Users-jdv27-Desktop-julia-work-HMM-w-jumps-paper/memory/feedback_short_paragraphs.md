---
name: No short paragraphs
description: User strongly dislikes short/orphan paragraphs - always merge into adjacent paragraphs
type: feedback
---

Never leave short paragraphs (1-3 sentences) standing alone. Always merge them into the preceding or following paragraph with a transition sentence.

**Why:** This is a strong stylistic pet peeve. The user has called them out multiple times across related work, methods, results, and discussion sections. Even a single orphan sentence paragraph is unacceptable.

**How to apply:** When editing any section of the paper, check for short paragraphs and proactively merge them. When removing content that leaves a paragraph stub, merge the remainder into its neighbor.
